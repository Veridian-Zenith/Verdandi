# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent, or participate somewhere else.

- Judge code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no digging through someone's history to win an argument. What you did elsewhere is not a reason to mistreat you here; what you do in this project's spaces is.
- Disagreement about kernels is expected and useful. Making it personal is not.
- The maintainer may remove comments, revoke access, or ban accounts for behavior in this project's spaces. Decisions can be discussed once, privately, then they stand.

This applies to the repository, its issue tracker, and any official chat.

Report problems: open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer directly.
